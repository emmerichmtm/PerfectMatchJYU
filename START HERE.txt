PerfectMatch JYU - local browser app for 64-bit Windows

1. Right-click PerfectMatchJYU-Windows.zip and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted PerfectMatch folder.
3. Double-click PerfectMatch.exe. Your browser opens automatically.
4. Click Try the included example, then Find matches.
5. Read the report, then download result.csv and report.txt.

For your own data, edit copies of the two CSVs in the templates folder.
Save as CSV UTF-8. Choose the files on the browser page and Find matches.
Keep the two input files with the two downloaded outputs in a dated folder.

No Python installation or internet connection is needed for matching.
The localhost address refers to your own computer. Files stay local.
Keep the _internal folder beside PerfectMatch.exe; do not move the EXE alone.

Click Close app before closing the browser tab to stop the local process.
Save your downloads first. Refreshing the page clears the displayed results.
If port 8765 is occupied, the app opens another available local address.
If Windows or your organisation blocks this unsigned app, ask IT to review it.

See manual.pdf for the illustrated-by-examples staff instructions, settings,
CSV columns, interpretation and troubleshooting. The same manual is linked
at the top of the browser page.
